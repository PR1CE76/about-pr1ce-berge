How to use this with Ray-Ban Meta glasses
  1. Pair the glasses with your phone in the Meta AI app. They play your phone's audio, including ChatGPT voice replies.
  2. Open this page on your phone and add it to your home screen so it launches like an app.
  3. Tap Speak, ask your question, then tap Open in ChatGPT. The question is already filled in on chatgpt.com. No account or paid plan is needed.
  4. In ChatGPT, tap the headphone or voice icon to hear the answer through the glasses.
  5. Shortcut: any link of the form ?q=your+question on this page pre-fills the box. Use it from an iPhone Shortcut or Android share sheet.
